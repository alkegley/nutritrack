GLP-1 + 1,250 Deficit Golden Rules:

  • Protect Muscle: Aim for at least 85g–100g protein daily to prevent metabolic slowdown.
  • Fiber Floor (≥ 25g): Slower gastric emptying means low food volume can cause constipation without daily fiber.
  • Hydrate Continuously: Delayed gastric emptying masks thirst; target 64+ oz of fluids daily to protect kidneys.
  • Electrolytes: Add a pinch of sea salt or electrolyte powder in water on days 1–3 to prevent postural dizziness.
  • Energy Guard: Weekly B12 shots bypass gastrointestinal absorption limits, keeping mitochondrial energy stable.
  • Rotate Sites: Alternating sides (Abdomen vs Thigh) optimizes absorption and prevents localized lipodystrophy.
